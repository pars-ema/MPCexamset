Modified Four Tank System – Control System Components

States (x):
- m₁, m₂, m₃, m₄ — masses of fluid in Tanks 1 through 4

Measurements (y):
- h₁, h₂ — measured fluid heights in Tank 1 and Tank 2 (since hᵢ = mᵢ / (ρ Aᵢ))

Manipulated Variables (MVs, u):
- F₁, F₂ — flow rates controlled by pumps into Tank 1 and Tank 2

Disturbance Variables (DVs):
- F₃, F₄ — unmeasured stochastic inflows into Tank 3 and Tank 4

Controlled Variables (CVs):
- z₁, z₂ — desired fluid heights in Tank 1 and Tank 2
